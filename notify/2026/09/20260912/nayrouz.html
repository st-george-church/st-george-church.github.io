A Blessed New Year to All

According to the Coptic and Ethiopian calendars, tomorrow marks the start of a unique, very short 13th month. This transitional month is called Nasie in Egypt and Pagume in Ethiopia, and it lasts for just five days. Immediately following it, we will celebrate the New Year on Friday, September 11. In the Coptic tradition, this joyful celebration is known as the Feast of Nayrouz and commemorates the martyrs of the Church.
