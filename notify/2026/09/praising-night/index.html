Dear Beloved Congregation,

Glory be to God.

Since our groundbreaking ceremony, we are grateful to share that, through the generosity of our congregation, we have collected $330,000 in loans and donations toward our $700,000 target.

This represents nearly 50% of our goal—an encouraging milestone made possible by every prayer, contribution, and act of faith.

We give thanks to God for His continued guidance and for the steadfast support of our church family. As we continue this important journey together, we humbly ask for your ongoing prayers and, where possible, your continued financial support in advance of our upcoming deadline of September 11th to pay the refundable deposit to the city.

May the Lord bless you and reward your generosity abundantly.

With gratitude,
St. George Coptic Orthodox Church
