Dear beloved congregation,

By the grace of God, we were blessed to gather together for the groundbreaking ceremony and cornerstone prayer for the new St. George Coptic Orthodox Church on July 26, 2026. Thank you to everyone who attended, served, prayed, helped organize, welcomed guests, guided parking, took photos, and contributed in any way to making this momentous occasion so special.

This was a historic and joyful milestone in the life of our church. After a long journey, we were able to stand together on the land, pray together, and witness the beginning of this next chapter for our parish. We are deeply grateful for your faith, perseverance, generosity, and love for the Church.

We are also collecting photos from the event so we can create a dedicated page on our church website to commemorate this special day. If you took photos during the groundbreaking ceremony or cornerstone prayer, please upload them to this Microsoft OneDrive site.

Your photos will help us preserve and share this beautiful moment with the entire congregation and with future generations of St. George and will be posted on the church website shortly.

As we look ahead, the contractor will continue with site preparation work this month. At the same time, we have an important financial milestone before us. The deadline to submit the required security deposit to the City is September 11, and we still need approximately $700,000 in donations or loans in order to secure the building permit and proceed to the next major step: pouring the foundation.

We kindly ask every family to prayerfully consider how they can support this urgent need. This is a critical step in keeping the project moving forward, and your continued support will help us reach the point where construction can advance from site preparation to the foundation of our new church.

May God bless every person who has supported this project through prayer, service, donations, loans, encouragement, and sacrifice. God has brought us to this milestone, and we trust He will continue to guide and strengthen us in all the work that lies ahead.

Thank you again for celebrating this blessed occasion with us. We will be posting more information about the groundbreaking event soon.

In Christ,
St. George Board & Planning Committee
