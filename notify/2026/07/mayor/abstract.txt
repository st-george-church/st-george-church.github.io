CITY OF SURREY

OFFICE OF THE MAYOR

Certificate of Recognition

St. George Coptic Orthodox Church

The Mayor and City Council present this certificate in recognition of the Groundbreaking Ceremony for our new church and in celebration of this significant milestone in your parish's journey.

Since the devastating fire, your community has demonstrated remarkable faith, resilience, and perseverance. Through years of prayer, careful planning, steadfast dedication and the unwavering efforts of your community, you have reached this momentous occasion. This groundbreaking marks not only the beginning of a new building but also a testament to the strength of your congregation and your shared commitment to preserving and growing your faith community for generations to come. On behalf of the City of Surrey, we wish you every success as construction progresses and look forward to celebrating the completion of your spiritual home.

July 26. 2026
Brenda Locke. Mayor
