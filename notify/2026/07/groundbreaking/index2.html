Dear beloved congregation,

As a reminder, tomorrow is the groundbreaking ceremony for the new St. George Coptic Orthodox Church.

The forecast is calling for rain, and we expect the site conditions to include mud, dust, and uneven ground. Please come prepared and dress appropriately for an outdoor construction-site setting.

Please note the following important reminders:

  • Bring an umbrella or rain jacket.
  • Wear appropriate footwear for wet, muddy, and uneven ground.
  • Women are kindly asked not to wear high heels or open-toed shoes of any kind.
  • Short boots are recommended.
  • There will be a tent (see attached image) but no seating except for elderly or anyone with physical limitations.
  • Please carpool where possible to limit the number of vehicles on site.
  • The ceremony is expected to take 1.5 hours starting at 1pm sharp.
  • Upon arrival, please listen carefully to the parking instructions provided by the ushers and follow their directions.
  • The ceremony will proceed as planned, and we look forward to gathering together for this blessed and historic milestone in the life of our church.

Thank you for your understanding and cooperation. May God bless this special day for our parish community.

In Christ,

St. George Board & Planning Committee
