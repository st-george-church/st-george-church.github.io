Dear St. George Congregation,

Further to our announcement regarding the Commemorative Medallion and Church Logo Design Contest, we are pleased to share some additional details.

Design Requirements

Participants are asked to design both sides of a circular commemorative medallion:

Front: A design representing St. George Coptic Orthodox Church, its name, St. George, faith, identity, and heritage. The winning front-side design will become the church's new official logo.

Back: A design commemorating the building of our new church and reflecting our vision for the church project. This may include artwork, symbols, a Bible verse, slogan, or other meaningful elements.

Both hand-drawn and digitally created designs are welcome. Designs should be suitable for professional production, engraving, or minting.

Judging Criteria

Designs will be judged based on:

  • Creativity and originality, including the extent to which the design reflects the participant’s own creative input rather than reliance on artificial intelligence (AI).
  • Connection to our Coptic Orthodox faith and and our church specifically.
  • Overall appearance and presentation.
  • Thought and meaning behind the design.
  • Suitability for production as a medallion and use of the front-side design as the church logo.

Prize

The winning participant will receive a $150 gift card. The medallion will be professionally produced and subsequently sold, with all proceeds supporting the church rebuilding project.

Submission Instructions

Please submit your designs for both sides of the medallion, along with your name and contact information, to:

fundraising@stgeorge.email

Deadline: November 30, 2026

By participating, participants grant St. George Coptic Orthodox Church the right to use, reproduce, modify, and publish their submitted designs for church-related purposes, including production of the medallion and use of the front-side design as the church’s official logo.

We encourage everyone to take part in this contest and help create a design that will commemorate the building of our new church and represent St. George Coptic Orthodox Church for years to come.
