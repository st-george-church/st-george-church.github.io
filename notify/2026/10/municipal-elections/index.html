Dearest Brothers and Sisters in Christ,

Advance voting for the upcoming municipal elections will begin this week in many communities across the Lower Mainland. We encourage all members of our church family to exercise their civic right and responsibility by casting their vote.

Every vote matters and has the power to make a meaningful difference in our communities. As you participate in the election process, we ask that you thoughtfully support candidates who are willing to help us pursue our vision of building a new church home for our congregation.

This is especially important for residents of Surrey, where we have been blessed to receive strong support from the City of Surrey. We are grateful for the cooperation and encouragement we have experienced thus far and pray that such support will continue in the future.

May God guide each of us in our decisions and bless our communities during this election season. We trust that He will lead us every step of the way.

God bless you and your families.

In Christ,

St. George Coptic Orthodox Church
